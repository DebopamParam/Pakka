# Avatar stack

Tiny overlapping initials; hovering one lifts it, dims the rest and filters what the card shows.

- 16px circles, initials at half size, pastel fill mixed toward the surface, 1.5px ring in the surface colour, -4px overlap.
- Hover: the avatar rises 2px and scales 1.12, the others fade to 35% (snappy spring 520/34).
- The parent uses the focused person to filter: rows not theirs fade, the footer says "Mara · 1 of 2 done".
