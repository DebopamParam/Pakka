# Testimonial wall

Two rows of quote cards drifting in opposite directions with feathered edges; hovering holds a row still.

## Anatomy
- Short centred headline ("Teams ship quieter.") and a muted intro.
- Two full-bleed marquee rows, 16px apart. Cards: 320px wide, 20px radius, white, hairline, card shadow, 20px padding.
  Quote at 14.5px in curly quotes; below it a 28px pastel initial disc, the name (medium) and role (muted), 12.5px.
- Keep quotes to one or two lines. Specific beats glowing ("done by lunch", not "amazing tool").

## Motion
- Rows run in opposite directions at slightly different speeds (50s / 56s per loop) so they never line up.
- Edges fade over 96px. Hover pauses that row. The section reveals once on scroll.
