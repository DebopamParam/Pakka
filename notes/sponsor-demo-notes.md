# Sponsor demo notes

Taken on the floor at Festive Hacks, 25 November 2026.

- **Organizers' live demo pattern:** they showed PayPal being turned on or off dynamically, based on system load or service status.
- **Problem statement first (NudgeBee founder, judge):** the problem statement matters most. Define the problem, then reverse-engineer the solution from it. Do not start from the tools and force a problem around them.
- **A clear "why" behind every component (NudgeBee founder, judge):** be ready to say why each tool or feature was chosen for this solution.
- **Architecture focus (NudgeBee founder, judge):** they explicitly want to evaluate the architecture of what you build.
- **Third-party platform integrations (NudgeBee founder, judge):** they highlighted how NudgeBee works with external platforms through third-party integrations.
